MUTUAL NON-DISCLOSURE AGREEMENT

This Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into as of May 1, 2026, by and between:

InnovateTech Inc., a Delaware corporation ("Party A")
and
Strategic Partners LLC, a California limited liability company ("Party B")

Collectively referred to as the "Parties."

WHEREAS, the Parties wish to explore a potential business relationship and will need to disclose certain confidential information to each other.

1. DEFINITION OF CONFIDENTIAL INFORMATION
"Confidential Information" means any information disclosed by either party that is:
- Marked as "Confidential" or similar designation
- Orally identified as confidential at time of disclosure and confirmed in writing within 15 days
- Would reasonably be understood as confidential given its nature

Confidential Information does NOT include information that:
- Is or becomes publicly available through no breach of this Agreement
- Was rightfully in receiving party's possession prior to disclosure
- Is independently developed without use of Confidential Information
- Is rightfully obtained from third party without restriction

2. OBLIGATIONS
Each party agrees to:
- Hold Confidential Information in strict confidence
- Use same degree of care as for own confidential information (minimum: reasonable care)
- Not disclose to third parties without prior written consent
- Use only for evaluation of potential business relationship
- Limit access to employees/advisors with need to know

3. PERMITTED DISCLOSURES
May disclose if required by law, court order, or government regulation, provided:
- Receiving party gives prompt written notice to disclosing party
- Cooperates with disclosing party's efforts to obtain protective order
- Discloses only minimum necessary

4. NO LICENSE
No license, ownership, or rights granted except as explicitly stated.
All Confidential Information remains property of disclosing party.

5. RETURN OF MATERIALS
Upon request or termination, receiving party shall:
- Return or destroy all Confidential Information and copies
- Certify in writing that all materials returned/destroyed

6. TERM
This Agreement effective May 1, 2026 and continues for 3 years.
Confidentiality obligations survive termination for 2 additional years (5 years total).

7. NO WARRANTY
Confidential Information provided "AS IS" without warranty.
Neither party obligated to enter business relationship.

8. REMEDIES
Breach may cause irreparable harm for which monetary damages inadequate.
Disclosing party entitled to injunctive relief in addition to other remedies.

9. MISCELLANEOUS
- Entire Agreement: Supersedes all prior agreements
- Amendment: Only in writing signed by both parties
- Governing Law: California law
- Severability: Invalid provisions severed, remainder enforceable
- No Waiver: Failure to enforce doesn't waive future enforcement
