INDEPENDENT CONTRACTOR AGREEMENT

This Agreement is entered into on March 1, 2026, between DataTech Solutions LLC ("Client") and Maria Rodriguez ("Contractor").

1. SERVICES
Contractor will provide data analytics consulting services as detailed in the attached Statement of Work.

2. COMPENSATION
Contractor shall be paid $150 per hour for services rendered. Invoices submitted bi-weekly. Payment due within 15 days of invoice receipt (NET 15).

3. LIABILITY
Contractor's total liability under this Agreement is limited to the amount of fees paid to Contractor in the six (6) months prior to any claim. Neither party shall be liable for consequential, indirect, or punitive damages.

4. INSURANCE
Contractor shall maintain professional liability insurance with minimum coverage of $500,000 per occurrence and $1,000,000 aggregate.

5. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY
Pre-existing IP: Each party retains ownership of their pre-existing IP.
Work Product: Client owns all deliverables created under this Agreement.
Tools/Methods: Contractor may reuse general tools, methods, and know-how developed.

6. CONFIDENTIALITY
Both parties agree to maintain confidentiality of proprietary information for 3 years following termination.

7. TERM AND TERMINATION
Initial term: 6 months starting March 1, 2026.
Either party may terminate with 30 days written notice.
Upon termination, Client pays for work completed through termination date.

8. INDEPENDENT CONTRACTOR
Contractor is an independent contractor, not an employee. Contractor responsible for own taxes, insurance, and benefits.

9. DISPUTE RESOLUTION
Disputes shall be resolved through binding arbitration in accordance with AAA Commercial Arbitration Rules. Venue: State of California.

10. NON-COMPETE
During term and for 6 months after, Contractor agrees not to provide similar services to Client's direct competitors listed in Exhibit B.

11. AMENDMENTS
Any amendments must be in writing and signed by both parties.

12. GOVERNING LAW
This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the State of California.
